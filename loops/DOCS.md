# Loops

Runs the health checks and the override mining once a day at `run_at`, and once at start if today's run has not happened. The panel charts the latest run and its findings, findings by severity, run time and overrides found per run, the automations the household overrides most, HVAC kWh per CDD65 by week and zone, each day's HVAC energy against its degree-days, and the manual-action record with its recorder gaps over 14 days; the findings stay listed with their detail, and every run is in a table below.

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| action | something to do: a Supervisor issue or unhealthy state, no backup holding Home Assistant or a stale one (full, or partial with Home Assistant; an add-on's own backup does not count), Uptime Kuma monitors down, safety devices or locks newly unavailable, a WAN newly offline |
| warn | worth a look: disk, a jump in unavailable entities, core log errors, an unsupported Supervisor, batteries newly under 20%, automation or script references to missing entities, an agent harness newly regressed (the Agent Harness Performance Tracker's regression sensor on, with the gates that fired), automations newly failing at run time and entities automations use newly unavailable (from Automation Monitor's sensors), custom integrations newly breaking now or within Breakage Radar's alert window, the agent-memory candidate review not run in 26 h, unable to reach its model or failing on a candidate, the options collector's weekday 16:20 New York run missing, aborted, with over 5% of symbols failed or unreadable, a check that failed; network items newly found (below) and network sources that could not be read |
| info | core and add-on updates, other updates newly available, newly unavailable entities, core log warnings, counts, Supervisor's `no_current_backup` (it counts full backups only, and Home Assistant's automatic backups are partial), custom integrations newly found by Breakage Radar using an API Home Assistant removes, with the earliest release; duplicate firewall policies or ones sharing a name, DNS to outside resolvers unblocked |

Safety devices, batteries, other updates, broken references, harness regressions and the Automation Monitor and Breakage Radar findings are reported only when new since the previous run; a regression is reported again when another gate fires, and a failing automation when its failing step changes. The monitors check needs the Automation Monitor and Breakage Radar integrations; without their sensors it reports itself failed. Monitors named in `kuma_ignore` are left out of the down check.

An override is a manual change to an entity within `override_window_s` after an automation changed it, to a different state. The ranking counts them per automation over the last 30 days with the number of times the automation fired.

`POST /api/run` on the ingress port runs everything now; an override the corpus already holds is not stored again. `GET /api/snapshot.json` reads the unavailable entities and the ERROR/WARNING log signatures live, and answers 502 when a read fails. `stop_signatures` counts the occurrences logged in the last 15 seconds before Core exited, the stop's own noise.

`memory_url` (absent means `http://10.20.52.10:8765`, empty turns it off) is the agent-memory add-on whose nightly candidate review the run reports from its `/health`: an info line with what it forgot and how many candidates are left for agents, always in the phone summary.

HVAC energy per cooling degree-day (loop 7). `hvac_zones` lists `name=statistic_id[,statistic_id]`, the energy statistics of every circuit that runs a zone (compressor and air handler, fan-only energy included); `outdoor_temperature` is a temperature statistic with hourly means. Each local day is complete when every expected hour (23 or 25 across a clock change) holds the temperature and every circuit; CDD65 and HDD65 are degree-hours over 24, and a cooling day has CDD65 of 5 or more and HDD65 under 1. A week's kWh per CDD65 is the sum of kWh over the sum of CDD65 on its complete cooling days, for each zone and for the days every zone counts, and needs five such days. Missing hours make a zone-day unavailable and are reported as a warning; nothing is filled in. `energy_meter` names the panel's total energy statistic: an hour it did not move is an hour the meter was not reporting, since statistics keep writing rows of zero change while it is offline, and every circuit's reading for that hour counts as missing. `controller_arm` labels the thermostat settings in force, recorded on each new day, so weeks under different settings are compared by arm. `controller_from` (empty: the last 30 days) is the first day measured; inputs are kept under `/data/controller`. Nothing here changes a thermostat.

Manual actions (loop 4). A websocket subscription keeps, per local day, every change of an actionable entity (lights, fans, climate, switches, locks, covers, media players, scenes, vacuums, input booleans) and every change a person made through Home Assistant, the controllable fields it changed recorded as the action. Connection gaps and time the add-on was not running are written down, and a day with one is not complete. The nightly run scores trivial policies on the later third of complete days: none, the most frequent action given the moment of an action, and an hour bucket (an action seen on two or more training days in the same weekday or weekend hour, predicted at every five-minute boundary of that hour), each with what it reproduced and its false positives. The score is provisional until 60 complete days exist.

`collector_url` (absent means `http://local-collector:8092`, empty turns it off) is the options collector add-on whose `/runs` the run reads: an info line for a good weekday run (a holiday run that only deduplicated is good), a warn when the run is missing, aborted or over 5% failed, or the collector cannot be read.

Network checks. `unifi_url` (absent means `https://10.20.52.1`, empty turns them off) is the UniFi OS gateway, read with `unifi_username` and `unifi_password`, a read-only local account without two-factor sign-in.

| Key | Item |
|---|---|
| `wan_down:<WAN>` | the WAN's status is offline, its link is down, or the gateway holds no state for it |
| `wan_degraded:<WAN>` | under 99% availability in the controller's uptime statistics |
| `wan_latency:<WAN>` | latency at least twice and 20 ms above the median of up to 14 previous nights, once five are held |
| `isolation:<network>` | a network in `isolated_networks` that UniFi does not isolate, whose first broad policy (every protocol, port, application and connection state, always) to the internal zone allows; user policies come before predefined ones, narrower ALLOW policies before it are its exceptions, and a block counts when it reaches every other network of the zone (any address, those networks, or addresses covering their subnets) |
| `camera_network:<mac>` | a UniFi Protect device, by MAC from Home Assistant's device registry, connected outside `camera_network` |
| `external_mac:<policy id>` | an ALLOW policy from the External zone matched on a MAC |
| `source_port:<policy id>` | a policy with a source port and no destination port, port group, application or web domain |
| `duplicate_policy:<policy id>` | a policy identical to an earlier one but for its name |
| `same_name:<policy id>` | a policy made separately (another index) under an earlier policy's name, matching something else |
| `dns_egress` | port 53 or 853 not blocked or rejected for TCP and UDP from all of the internal zone to any address in the External zone |
| `port_forward:<id>` | an enabled port forward |
| `upnp` | UPnP on the gateway |

An item is reported when its key is new or its value changed since the previous run; the first run reports everything current. A policy item's value is a digest of what the policy matches and does, so a policy changed under the same id is reported again. A rule that cannot decide an item in a run (its source unread, a field missing, a device not connected) keeps the item as it was. Each finding names its items' keys as `dismiss: <key>`; keys in `network_dismiss` are not reported. A source that cannot be read is a warn every run, and the items of its rules stay as they were. `/api/network.json` holds the current items, the dismissed keys, the WAN readings, the latency history and the field names read from the controller.
